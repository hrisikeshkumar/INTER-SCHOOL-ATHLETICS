How to Publish to Google Play Store (APK / AAB):

  1. Host over HTTPS: Deploy this single HTML file on Firebase Hosting, Vercel, or Netlify.
  2. Generate Signed Android App Bundle (.AAB / .APK): Use PWABuilder.com or Google's Bubblewrap CLI (Trusted Web Activity - TWA) or Capacitor to wrap your HTTPS URL into a signed Play Store package.
  3. Google Play Console: Upload the generated .aab file, add your Digital Asset Links (assetlinks.json), and publish to the Play Store.
